# Classes

1. every class is identified by a year, in romans, and a letter. For example, IIIA or IIB
2. there can be as many classes the school may decide
3. different years may have more or less letters wrt to other years
4. a class as a list of subject matters, which might differs from the list of other classes
5. matters assigned to classes might have different requirements wrt the original matter. Requirements must then be placed in the Class-Matter assignment
